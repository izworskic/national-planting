How the calendar decides your dates

More than a hardiness zone

USDA hardiness zones describe winter minimums. This calendar instead starts with local NOAA freeze climatology, then applies crop maturity, cold tolerance, soil-temperature, heat and succession rules.

Fall timing is calculated

Where frost matters, fall sowing is worked backward from local cold-risk anchors with crop-specific maturity, slowdown and safety time. In hot or nearly frost-free climates, cool-season crops shift toward fall, winter and early spring.
