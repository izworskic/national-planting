Can I still plant spring bulbs?

Decide whether tulips, daffodils, crocus, hyacinths and alliums can still be planted using actual soil readings and local seasonal context.

Soil temperature comes from your thermometer, never inferred from air temperature.

Change an input to update your decision. This tool does not forecast when your specific bed freezes.

Why the decision works this way

Wisconsin Extension recommends planting spring-flowering bulbs in approximately 50–55°F soil before it freezes. Warmer winter areas can require species- and cultivar-specific chilling.

Read university extension planting guidance.

Official context: NOAA climate normals and NWS weather data. Historical frost dates are regional, not a soil temperature measurement.
